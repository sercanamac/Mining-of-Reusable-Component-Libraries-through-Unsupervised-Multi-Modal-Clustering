Bentley3D — experiment data bundle
==================================
Unzip at the repository ROOT (folder containing notebooks/, data/, processed_data/).
Paths are repo-relative and drop back into place.

Included:
  processed_data/features/                     baseline OBB parquet (Bentley)
  processed_data/rendered_features/            visual embeddings (DINOv3/SigLIP/DuoDuo/Gemini)
  processed_data/descriptions/                 VLM descriptions (TF-IDF for catalog)
  processed_data/gemini_embeddings*/           text embeddings v1-v7
  processed_data/gemma4_embeddings/            text embeddings (Gemma4 / IFCNet)
  processed_data/meshes/train/<gid>.obj        the 1,849 annotated-subset meshes
  data/IFCNetCore/features/  data/IFCNetCore/processed/   IFCNet baseline + embeddings
  data/metadata.json                           GlobalId -> IfcType -> mesh_filename map
  data/annotated_metadata.json                 rich per-object IFC metadata (subset)
  data/annotated_subset.json                   the annotated subset allow-list (1,849)
  notebooks/data_analysis/results/feature_engineering/engineered_features.parquet

NOT included (only needed to regenerate from scratch): raw IFC submissions,
full processed_data/meshes (non-subset), processed_data/renders*, IFCNetCore meshes/renders.
